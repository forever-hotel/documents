## Status and use

These are evidence-based retrospective drafts for team review, not fabricated meeting minutes. Sprints 5-7 use the board dates supplied in the chat. Sprint 8 reviews actual quality/release work by theme, including work the team placed under Sprint 7; it is not an assertion that a separate dated Sprint 8 has finished.

Each report includes Went Well, To Improve, Action Items and Metrics, following Appendix B.2. Observed closure counts are explicitly separated from committed/completed scope and velocity. Missing attendance, meeting dates, story points and sprint-wide CI results remain unverified. Owners/due points in the action tables are recommendations, not recorded historical promises.

The guideline describes four-week development sprints; the supplied board uses two-week windows. Record the team's agreed explanation or supervisor-approved mapping before final submission.
